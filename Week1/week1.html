A Folden Silver: DEFEMNHS educators showcase Agoo culture in Dance of Colors.

Educators from Don Eufemio F. Eriguel Memorial National High School, proudly representing Cluster IV (Agoo West, Agoo East, Aringay, and Caba), captured 1st Runner-Up in the Dance of Colors: A Variety Show Competition held on November 20, 2026, at the Speaker Pro-Tempore Francisco I. Ortega Convention Center. Staged as one of the highlights of the La Union Provincial Education Summit 2026, the competition gathered educators across the province to celebrate artistry, camaraderie, and local heritage. Set to the lively rhythm of the Ilocano folk classic Pamulinawen, the DEFEMNHS contingent delivered a vibrant performance showcasing the rich cultural identity of Agoo, prominently featuring the town’s famed Dinengdeng Festival. The winning dance troupe was composed of:

    Mr. Junelle H. Verial
    Ms. Erika R. Fangonilo
    Mr. Richard Von G. Tejada
    Ms. Russell Mae M. Sapla
    Mr. Kismoore D. Padua
    Ms. Marilou M. Domondon
    Mr. Rodrigo O. Romero
    Ms. Jennilyn B. Ramirez
    Mr. Ray Karlo J. Carrera
    Ms. Antonette Lou M. Salom
Behind the routines and production was the management team of Alvin O. Doctolero, Brenda Lyn B. Hernandez, and Catherine M. Romero, under the leadership of School Principal IV Dr. Adela C. Libunao. The school also extends its heartfelt gratitude to Dr. Luisito Libatique, Public Schools District Supervisor, whose steadfast encouragement and support helped propel the Agoo delegation to a podium finish. Appreciation and heartfelt gratitude is also given to the Municipality of Agoo, led by Hon. Mayor Frank O. Sibuma, for the assistance and support provided to the entire DEFEMNHS contingent. DEFEMNHS joins 4th Quarter NSED, leads disaster readiness drill

Don Eufemio F. Eriguel Memorial National High School (DEFEMNHS) joined the nation in participating in the 4th Quarter Nationwide Simultaneous Earthquake Drill (NSED) to strengthen disaster preparedness and resilience among students and staff. Led by the School Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (SDRRM) coordinators, Mr. Cesar Tubera and Mr. Francious Mitchelle Fangonilo, learners actively demonstrated the proper safety procedures before, during, and after an earthquake. During the alarm, students executed the "Duck, Cover, and Hold" protocol before calmly proceeding to the designated evacuation area on the DEFEMNHS open grounds. Following the drill, a post-evaluation activity was conducted at the Agoo Farmers' Gymnasium to assess the execution and identify key areas for continuous improvement in safety protocols. Under the leadership of Dr. Adela C. Libunao, DEFEMNHS remains steadfast in fostering a safe school community through proactive disaster preparedness drills and key safety initiatives.
